Thank You

To everyone who has been part of Global View,

Thank you for sharing a small piece of your world with me.

Every postcard, message, photograph, and story that reaches me carries more than words. It may offer a glimpse of a city, a landscape you love, a place that holds special meaning, or simply a few lines written during an ordinary day. Somehow, these small gestures make the world feel a little closer.

Global View exists because of these connections.

I created this space to collect and share moments born from conversations across borders — different languages, places, and lives, yet often united by the same curiosity about the world and one another.

Whether you sent me a postcard, shared a photograph, wrote me a message, or simply stopped by this page:

thank you for being here.

This little corner of my website would not exist without you.

Perhaps that’s what makes exchanging postcards so special:

the world is enormous, yet sometimes it can fit into a small envelope.

With gratitude,

Nanjing, China

谢谢你

致所有参与过 Global View 的人：

谢谢你愿意与我分享你所在世界的一小部分。

每一张寄来的明信片、每一条消息、每一张照片和每一个故事，所带来的都不只是文字。它可能让人看到一座城市、一片你喜欢的风景、一处对你有特殊意义的地方，又或者只是某个普通日子里写下的几句话。不知不觉间，这些小小的分享，让这个世界好像离我们更近了一点。

Global View 正是因为这些连接而存在。

我创建这个空间，是想收集并分享那些因跨越国界的交流而诞生的片段——不同的语言、地方和生活，却常常因为我们对世界以及彼此的好奇而联系在一起。

无论你是给我寄来了一张明信片、分享了一张照片、写了一条消息，还是只是路过这个页面：

谢谢你来到这里。

如果没有你，这个网站中的这一小块空间也不会存在。

或许，这正是明信片交换如此特别的地方：

世界如此辽阔，却有时可以装进一个小小的信封里。

谨致感谢，

中国，南京
