Before you share, there are a few things you should know

1. How to send your photo

When you click Send below, the website will copy a prepared email message and open your device's default mail app. After reading the information below, please click “I have read and understood the Photo Submission License Agreement” at the bottom of this page to enable Send.

All you need to do is attach the photo you would like to share, fill in the information requested in the message, and send the email.

2. A few things worth knowing

  1. By sending a photo to me, you are giving permission for me to display it on my website. Don't worry, though — I will not use the photo for anything outside this website.
  2. Other people may be able to download the photo you send. I will remind visitors to respect your copyright, but some things cannot be completely prevented. I hope you understand.
  3. Your privacy matters.
    1. Global View mainly collects landscapes, architecture, nature, culture, and similar content photographed by people in different countries.
    2. Please do not send me private information or anything that should not be made public.
    3. If you ever want your photo removed from the website, just contact me and I will take it down as soon as possible.
  4. If I feel that a photo seriously does not meet the requirements for Global View, I will reply to you by email. I reserve the right not to upload photos that do not meet the requirements, although I hope we never have to do that.

That's all. Wishing you all the best and a lovely day.
